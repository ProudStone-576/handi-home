The house

Cooked in the pot, served with warmth.

A handi is the round, deep cooking pot at the heart of North Indian kitchens — wide-bellied and narrow-necked, made for slow simmering that lets gravies thicken and spices settle in. It's the idea this neighbourhood restaurant is named for.

Guests talk most about two things: the food is delicious, and the vibe is nice. Come in with family or friends, pick up on your way home, or have it delivered without a knock.
